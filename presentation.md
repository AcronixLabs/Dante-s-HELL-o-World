# "Hello World" is broken

It's too simple. Too boring. Too normal.

So we made it unreasonably complicated.

--

# What we built

A proof-of-work "Hello World" pipeline:

- 1-qubit quantum simulator per character
- SHA-256 mining with difficulty nonces
- Chained blockchain blocks per character

<!--

- Fictional: Klingon, Dune's Galach, Minionese, Dovahzul…
- Esolangs: Brainfuck, Malbolge, Chicken, LOLCODE, Chef

# Printing one letter

Enterprise bureaucracy, one character at a time:

- `d.cpp` — 400 lines of C++ templates to print `d`
- `e.rs` — 400 lines of Rust committees to print `e`
- `h.asm` — 15 phases of x86-64 assembly to emit `H`
- `o.chef` — molecular gastronomy for `O`

# The lesson

Learn by overcomplicating.

If you can mine "Hello World" on a quantum blockchain in 13 languages…

…the real Hello World is easy.

Absurdity is the best teacher.
